Hello, we're ISNESI!

A world where every child has room to grow.

ISNESI (Inclusive Special Needs Education & Social Innovation) works with children, families, educators and communities to build a more inclusive society. We focus on strengths, remove barriers and make participation possible.

Our vision

To build an inclusive and equitable society where every child and person with a disability has equal access to quality education, opportunities, dignity, and full participation in community life.

Our mission

To promote inclusive and special needs education through innovative, accessible, and community-driven solutions that empower children with disabilities and other vulnerable children to learn, thrive, and participate fully in society.

What we do

Little steps. Big possibilities.

We bring people together around six areas of action that help children learn, participate and thrive.

📚

Inclusive Education

Promoting equal access to quality education for children with disabilities.

💚

Special Needs Support

Providing educational, psychosocial, and developmental support tailored to individual needs.

💡

Social Innovation

Developing practical and sustainable solutions to barriers faced by persons with disabilities.

🌱

Capacity Building

Training teachers, parents, caregivers, communities, and stakeholders on inclusive practices.

📣

Advocacy & Awareness

Challenging discrimination, stigma, and exclusion while promoting disability rights.

🤝

Partnerships

Working with government, schools, communities, NGOs, OPDs, and other stakeholders to advance inclusion.
