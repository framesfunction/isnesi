## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The public surface feels authored for ISNESI, not category-interchangeable. The disability-forward framing ("Disability is part of human diversity", "keeping each child's dignity at the centre"), honest live counts with explicit "not projections" disclaimers, and the safeguarding-first enquiry form are specific to ISNESI's mission. The design system (PageHero, Section, CtaBand, EmptyNote, signature cards, chips) is consistently applied across 13 of 14 content pages.

The homepage is the outlier: it uses an inline hero instead of the `PageHero` component that every other page uses, and previously carried four decorative blobs plus two emoji callouts (now fixed). After the recent polish pass, the homepage aligns closely with the rest of the surface.

**Deterministic scan:** `impeccable detect --json` returned `[]` — no findings. Exit code 0 (clean). No false positives.

## Overall Impression

What works: honest metrics, dignity-centred copy, consistent design-system application, safeguarding-first messaging. What doesn't: motion scale inconsistency across pages (`-translate-y-0.5` vs `-translate-y-1`) and the homepage hero drift from `PageHero`. The single biggest opportunity: unify motion and consolidate the homepage onto the shared `PageHero` component so the whole surface reads as one system.

## What's Working

1. **Consistent design-system application.** PageHero, Section, CtaBand, EmptyNote, signature cards, and chips follow `DESIGN.md` on 13 of 14 content pages. Empty states are never hidden — every empty state uses `EmptyNote` with an honest message and a path forward.
2. **Honest, trust-building metrics.** Homepage, Impact, and Partners pages all show live counts with explicit disclaimers ("Counts reflect what is published on this website today, not projections"). Donate page is transparent that online giving isn't open yet.
3. **Safeguarding-first copy.** The contact page explicitly routes child-protection concerns to emergency services rather than the general enquiry form. Get Involved explains what happens to messages ("A person reads it — there is no automated reply.").

## Priority Issues

**[P2] Motion scale inconsistency across the public surface.** Hero CTAs use `hover:-translate-y-0.5` on donate, get-involved, impact, kids-corner, and not-found pages, while signature cards use `hover:-translate-y-1` (per `DESIGN.md`). The homepage was fixed to `-translate-y-1` in the last polish pass but other pages weren't. Fix: unify all hero CTA hover lifts to `-translate-y-1` to match cards. Suggested command: `$impeccable layout` on the public page files.

**[P2] Homepage hero drift from PageHero component.** All other public pages delegate the hero to `PageHero` (eyebrow, title, description, up to two actions). The homepage uses an inline duplicate with its own spacing and motion values. Fix: migrate the homepage hero to `PageHero` to eliminate component drift. Suggested command: `$impeccable layout` or `$impeccable polish`.

**[P3] Emoji in CTA label ("Make a difference 🧡") on donate page.** Screen readers announce "heart emoji" as part of the CTA text, adding noise. User chose to keep the playful tone, so the emoji stays — but consider wrapping with accessible labelling or keeping it decorative-only. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify` or `$impeccable quieter`.
